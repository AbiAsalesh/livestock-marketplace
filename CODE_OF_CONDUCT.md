# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior
Be kind, constructive, patient, and respectful. Give feedback about ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent and help beginners learn.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information are not welcome.

## Enforcement
Report problems privately to the project maintainers through the repository contact options. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
